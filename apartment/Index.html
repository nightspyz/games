Walk mode

  1. Tap Walk to drop into the entrance hall at eye level (160 cm), then use the room buttons along the top to jump straight into any room.
  2. Phone: the joystick at the bottom‑left moves you; drag anywhere on the picture to look around.
  3. Computer: W A S D or the arrow keys move, hold Shift to hurry, drag with the mouse to look, Esc or the Walk button to leave.
  4. Walls stop you; doorways are open so every room can be entered. The time slider keeps working while you walk (reopen it by leaving walk mode).
  5. Lighting is physical in spirit: the sun enters only through the windows and the balcony glass (an invisible ceiling blocks light from above), and after dark the rooms are lit only by their own fixtures, each a real spot light with its own shadow map. Nothing is baked: a faint bounce term is the only non-shadowed light, standing in for inter-reflection.
  6. A robot vacuum roams the apartment, bumping off walls and furniture; its shadow updates live under the sun and the nearest fixture.
  7. The viewer sandbox cannot download external 3D model files, so all furniture is modelled in the page with rounded, bevelled forms.
