Free Surf

Pick up your board on the beach, carry it into the water, step on, paddle out, then stand up and let the waves carry you to shore.

  • VR: grip / trigger near the board to pick it up, let go to drop it. Step onto it while it floats.
  • Lie down on the floor to paddle: pull your arms back in strokes. One arm stronger = turn.
  • Kneel = sit up (slow), stand = surf. Waves shove you shoreward while you stand. Lean or use the thumbstick to steer.
  • Face button (A/B/X/Y) bails off the board. Thumbstick walks on the beach.
  • Desktop: W/S walk & A/D turn, E grab/drop, Space lie/stand, W paddle, Q bail.
