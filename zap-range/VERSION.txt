Zap Range VR - version 18 (2 Oct 2026)

Host index.html on any https address and open it in the Quest 3 browser.
Everything is in this one file: no assets folder needed.

New in 18:
- Wrist menu: fixed the top buttons looking greyed out (the dark backing was being drawn over them)
- Wrist menu buttons react faster: Start round and Night mode trigger on a quick tap,
  Reset table / Reset score almost as fast; Exit VR keeps a longer hold so you can't leave by accident

New in 17:
- You can't walk or step past the pink firing line; a neon force field fades in as you get close

Also in this build:
- Neon arcade look: blacklight carpet, neon tubes and wall art, party lights, mirror ball
- Solid hologram targets with scanlines, glow and floor projectors
- RAILGUN smart sight: locks on when charged (red + beep) and fires by itself on a sure hit
- LOBBER sight with 5-25 m drop marks; turns white when the shot will hit
- Timed 60 s rounds with saved best score, drones that shoot back, mini / armour / gold targets
- REPEATER overheat, RAILGUN charge-up and two-handed grip, hip holsters, night mode, wrist menu
