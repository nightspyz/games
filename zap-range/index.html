Zap Range

Open this page in the Quest 3 browser and press Enter VR. Five ray guns are laid out on the bench in front of you.

Controls
Grip
Reach for a gun’s handle with either hand and hold the grip to pick it up, let go to drop it. Let go next to your hip to holster it
Trigger
Fire
Left stick
Walk. You can't cross the pink firing line: a force field lights up as you get close
Right stick
Turn
A or X
Put all guns back on the bench
B or Y
Go back to the bench
Wrist menu
Turn your left palm toward your face. Hold your right hand on a button: Start round, Night mode, Reset table, Reset score or Exit VR
Both sticks
Click both in together to exit VR
Weapons
ZAPPER
Classic single-shot blaster
REPEATER
Hold the trigger for rapid fire. Watch the heat gauge on the back: if it overheats it locks up until it cools
SPREADER
Fires a wide spray of 8 bolts
RAILGUN
Hold the trigger to charge. Once charged, its sight locks onto the target nearest your aim, turns red and beeps, then fires by itself as soon as the shot is certain to hit. Releasing the trigger fires straight away. Grab the glowing foregrip with your other hand to aim with both hands
LOBBER
Arcing plasma ball that blows up everything nearby. Use the 5–25 m drop marks on its sight; the sight turns white when the shot will hit a target
Targets
Balloon
20 points. Gold ones are worth 30 and slow time down for 5 seconds
Archery
5 to 100 points depending on the ring
Mini
Small pink flying target, double points
Armour
Steel plate that takes 3 hits, 60 points
Drone
Shoots slow red orbs at you: dodge or shoot them down. 80 points, getting hit costs 50

On a computer: drag to look around, click to shoot, keys 1–5 switch weapon. Now firing: ZAPPER
