Lantern Tide - version 5.1 (6 Oct 2026)

Host this folder on any https address and open index.html in the Quest browser (WebXR needs https).
Everything is in index.html; three.js r128 is loaded from cdnjs.

New in 5.1:
- Fixed everything looking blown out white on some graphics cards: a sky shader line used smoothstep with its edges the wrong way round,
  which is undefined in GLSL (fine on some GPUs, NaN on others, and NaN draws as white). Two such lines fixed.
- The far sea (the planet's surface at sea level) now fades into exactly the same haze colour the old far ocean did.
- The cabin's weathered wood is lighter. A new /about page (about/index.html) tells the story of the game, with screenshots.

New in 5.0: THE WORLD IS A PLANET
- The whole world now curves like a small planet: the sea, the land, trees, boats, everything drops away below the horizon (very gently at
  sea level, so islands still rise over it as you approach). Climb, and the curvature grows until you can see the planet is round.
- Fly up to space: the sky fades slowly from blue to black, the clouds lie low (you climb through them and they are beneath you), a thin
  atmosphere hugs the ground, and above about 10 km it is black with stars only. From space you look down at your planet: islands with
  their shallows, clouds with shadows, a day side and a dark side with a soft terminator, the sun's glint on the sea, city, airport and
  lava lights on the night side, an atmosphere glowing blue at the limb and orange at sunset, the real sun and the moon (with phases)
  and a few neighbouring planets. The planet turns beneath you as you travel.
- Flight is much faster: Slow 25, Normal 90, Fast 350, Rocket 1800, Warp 7000 m/s (choose in the Travel tab). You can fly out over the
  edge of the world, and the islands stop streaming while you are high up.
- The islands are no longer evenly spaced: every row of the layout is shifted by its own amount, islands cluster into archipelagos with wide
  empty seas between, small islets are more common, and the world is a little bigger (7 x 7 cells).

New in 4.2:
- Roads now rest on top of the landscape (Coastline's road settling): each point sits on the highest ground under the road's width,
  smoothed so it bridges dips and rivers, with its sides dropping down to the ground, so it never disappears into hills.
- Temple/shrine islands have no roads and no wind turbines (and neither does any island that holds a forest shrine).
- The minimap sits further in toward the middle of your view.

New in 4.1:
- Buildings are solid and lit the right way round (the normals/winding were inverted); you cannot walk or fly into them (box
  colliders, roof height respected). Window lights, street lights, runway lights, the oil rig's work lights and the volcano glow now show
  from far away at night (Coastline's glowing points, unaffected by haze).
- More of Coastline's sea and sky: ships (sailboat, tour boat, ferry, tanker, cruise liner, container ship, coast guard) cruising in
  open water and round islands; an oil rig (with a flare, a helipad and a deck you can stand on); wind farms at sea and wind turbines
  on high ground; a flock of gulls that circles the leaping fish and dives; a sightseeing plane circling the island you are at, and
  airliners crossing high overhead. Teleport: nearest oil rig / wind farm. Cliffs and fields were already in 4.0.
- Island shores: sandy beaches with boulders at the waterline, sea stacks, driftwood, shrubs, palms right down to the beach, kelp
  forests, and (as at home) corals, reef rocks and fish schools wherever you swim; leaping fish follow you to any island.
- The minimap now floats in your view, up in the left corner, instead of sitting on your hand.
- Festival lanterns (sky lanterns, lotus on the sea, the beach garland) are now OFF by default; turn them on in the World tab.

New in 4.0: THE WIDER WORLD
- A world of about 6.5 km across, made of islands that stream in as you travel (nothing is loaded at start): tropical islands with
  forests and rivers, volcanoes (smoking, with lava flows), temple islands, swamps with mangroves, cities with lit windows, an
  airport island with planes landing and taking off, plus islets and big islands of every size. Many places are open sea only. The islands sit on a
  staggered, jittered layout rather than a grid. Same seed, same world (add ?seed=anything to the address for a different one).
- Islands rise out of the sea as you approach: the land bends away below the horizon, and distant islands are built in the haze.
- Some islands have a coast road with power lines, guard rails where the ground falls away, bridges over rivers, and cars (with
  headlights at night); some have farmland (Coastline's field patchwork, with a barn, silo and hay bales); some have sea cliffs.
- Hand-made places of interest set into the land: a fishing cabin with a pier, dinghy and lantern; an abandoned campsite with a cold fire
  and a diary; a forest shrine with a gate, stone lanterns and an offering. Their ground is levelled and blended, paths lead to them,
  trees keep clear of them, and what you do there is remembered (even in later visits). See WORLD_DESIGN.md.
- Minimap (M cycles its size; on your left controller in VR), the nearest island is named at its foot. Teleport tab: nearest of each
  island type and place of interest. New Travel tab: flight on/off and speed, boat speed, and "Return the boat to the dock".

New in 3.12:
- Drive the boat (ported from Coastline's boat.js): press Y on the left controller (or the Y key) within a few metres of the
  boat, or while swimming next to it, to take the helm; press Y again to step away, anywhere at sea. At the helm the left stick is
  the throttle (forward / back) and the rudder (left / right); on a screen W S A D. It has acceleration and drag, pivots slowly when
  stopped, rides the waves, slides along shallows, the pier and the breakwater rocks, and has an engine sound. If you leave
  it, you are carried along the deck while it coasts. The Teleport tab has "Board the boat".
- Reef rocks and corals no longer look see-through underwater.

New in 3.11:
- Flight, VRChat style (World tab, on by default): jump (A), then hold a trigger in the air and you fly the way that controller
  points, up to about 7 m/s; use both hands and it blends them. Let go and you carry on a little, then fall. Falling into deep water
  drops you straight into swimming. On a screen: jump, then hold Space in the air to fly where you look.

New in 3.10:
- A Teleport tab in the X menu: Beach (start), Campfire, Lighthouse, Rocks at sea (the breakwater boulders, which you can now stand
  on), End of the dock, and the Island hilltop. The menu closes and you arrive facing the place.

New in 3.9:
- The VR menu now stays put relative to you: it is placed in front of you when you press X and then moves with you as you walk,
  swim or turn, instead of staying behind at a spot in the world.

New in 3.8:
- More natural floating: treading water, your eyes sit a hand's breadth above the surface (set from your real height), you rise
  and fall with each wave and bob gently; a big wave can wash over your head.

New in 3.7:
- Swim with your arms: in deep water hold a trigger and sweep that hand through the water (pull it back, down or sideways);
  the water pushes you the opposite way. Use both hands for stronger strokes; you glide and slow between strokes, and your
  controllers buzz with each pull. The stick still swims too.

New in 3.6:
- The X menu now has three tabs: Time & weather, World, Performance.
- World tab: aurora on/off, star brightness (Dim to Brilliant), wave height (Calm to Huge), sand-mark detail (Low / Medium / High),
  and a button to smooth the sand. Your choices are remembered.
- Marks in the sand are now dug in (hollows with damp, packed sand at the bottom) rather than raised.
- Better rocks: layered stone with cracks, mica flecks and real surface relief, flat fracture faces, on the beach, lighthouse
  boulders, breakwater and fire ring.
- You can jump onto rocks (A / Space): rocks are solid surfaces now, you step up about half a metre and jump for more.
- Swimming: wade out past your chest and you swim, moving the way you look (look down to dive, up to rise, A kicks you up).
  Underwater has its own look and sound: teal murk, a bright window to the sky above, muffled audio, a seabed all around the island.
  You can swim out to the harbour and climb out onto the dock or boat.

New in 3.5:
- Calmer, more natural sunsets and sunrises: orange is now the dominant colour (gold to orange to a soft peach, fading to blue),
  with the deep reds and purples toned right down, in the sky, the clouds and the sun.
- Smoother clouds and god rays. They are no longer marched for every pixel with a random start (which left grain);
  they are drawn into a small map of the whole sky, a quarter of it per frame, and read back with a soft blur.
  The cost no longer depends on the screen, so the quality levels are cheap: defaults are now Medium clouds and
  Medium god rays. Low / Medium / High set the sky map size (512 / 768 / 1024 wide) and the march steps.

New in 3.4:
- Performance settings in the X menu, for the two costliest parts of the picture: the raymarched clouds
  (Off / Low / Medium / High: march steps, light samples, fine edge detail) and the god rays (Off / Low / Medium / High),
  plus whether clouds shade the sea and sand. Lower defaults now: Low clouds, Low god rays, no cloud shadows.
  Your choices are remembered in the browser.
- A live frame-rate and frame-time reading in the menu (green 60+, amber 40+, red below).

New in 3.3:
- A menu on X (VR: X on the left controller, point and pull the trigger; screen: X) to set the time of day
  (drag the bar, or Dawn / Noon / Dusk / Night), how fast time passes (pause / 1x / 4x / 16x) and the weather
  (Clear / Cloudy / Rain / Storm / Fog / Mist, or Random to let the forecast take over again).
- The drone controller's readout no longer lies over its screen: the picture and the text have their own areas.

New in 3.2:
- The drone controller takes the place of your hand when you hold it (bigger, so the screen is easy to read).
- Real drone-remote controls: left stick forward / back = up / down, left / right = rotate;
  right stick forward / back = fly forward / back, left / right = strafe.
- Press A to jump.
- The drone flies itself home if you leave it for 45 s; after 90 s it is put back on its pad,
  fully charged, and the controller goes back on the table.

New in 3.1:
- Fireworks from Coastline: a crate of rockets on the beach. Pick one up, light it (trigger, or touch the fuse
  to the campfire) and it shoots up. Set one down and it plants upright and flies straight up.
- Drone from Coastline: a model on a landing pad and a hand controller with a live camera screen.
  VR: sticks fly it, trigger takes off / lands. Screen: WASD, R / F, drag, Space.
- One shared pick-up system for the stick, rockets and controller.
- Click the left stick to toggle run mode.

New in 3.0:
- Coastline's reef (7 coral kinds), kelp forests, fish schools, leaping fish and cabin cruiser.
- A small dock with the boat moored alongside, in a sheltered, dredged basin behind a breakwater.
- Coastline's weather: forecast cycle plus fog days. Random weather and time of day on each visit.

New in 2.1:

New in 2.0 (the island rework):
